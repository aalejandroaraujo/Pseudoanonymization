Manual Image Blur Regions

Manually select areas in embedded images to blur

What is this?

PDFs often contain embedded images (screenshots, photos, logos) that may include sensitive text. OCR (text recognition) automatically blurs text matching your deny list, but it can miss some text due to fonts, colors, or image quality.

Use this section to manually mark areas you want blurred in addition to OCR detection.

Example: A screenshot shows "Project Alpha" as a logo. OCR might detect the text "Project" but miss "Alpha" due to stylized font. Click on the image and draw a rectangle over the logo to ensure it gets blurred.
